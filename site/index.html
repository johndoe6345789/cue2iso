CUE/BIN to ISO

Turns a CD image (.cue + .bin) into a standard .iso. It runs in your browser: the files are read here and never uploaded.

    What it does

    A raw CD sector is 2352 bytes, of which 2048 are the data. The converter keeps those 2048 bytes of every data-track sector and drops the sync, header and error-correction bytes. It supports MODE1/2352, MODE2/2352, MODE2/2336 and 2048-byte tracks. Audio tracks can't go in an ISO and are skipped. When the cue has several data tracks you get one ISO per track.

    Mixed-mode discs (data track plus CD audio) give you just the data. Copy-protected discs and anything with bad sectors can't be made into a faithful ISO.

    Very large images (several hundred MB) need that much free memory in browsers without the "save file" API (Firefox, Safari, phones). Desktop Chrome and Edge write straight to disk.
